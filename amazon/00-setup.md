# Setup, in order

About a week of evenings. Claude can walk you through every step except the ones marked **you**: anything with a login, an ID, a bank detail or a signature is yours to type. Claude never types a password, a tax ID or a bank number, and never stores one in a file.

Prices below were checked on 2026-11-12. They change, so check the linked page before you pay.

## The steps

**1. You: a personal Claude plan for this business.**
- Cost: Pro is $20 a month billed monthly ([claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-12).
- Why: the company's Claude account belongs to the company. Business ideas, supplier names and numbers you put there are theirs to see.

**2. Run as a sole owner under your own name for now.**
- Cost: $0.
- Why: no California entity fee. The LLC comes later, once profit is real (see `amazon-vs-options-california.md`).

**3. You: get an EIN at irs.gov.**
- Cost: $0. The IRS says it is free and warns about sites that charge for it ([IRS: get an EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)).
- Why: a federal tax ID for supplier forms, so your Social Security number stays off them.

**4. You: open a separate bank account and card for the business.**
- Cost: usually $0.
- Why: one account in, one account out. Bookkeeping becomes ten minutes a month instead of a weekend.

**5. You: open Amazon Seller Central on the Professional plan.**
- Cost: $39.99 a month ([sell.amazon.com/pricing](https://sell.amazon.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-12). The Individual plan is $0.99 per item sold, but it lists one product at a time and wholesale needs Professional's bulk tools, ads and API access.
- Why now: the identity check can take days to a few weeks, so start early. Have your ID, the business bank account, a card and your tax info ready.

**6. Seller's permit: skip it if you sell only on Amazon.**
- Cost: $0.
- Why: California does not require a seller's permit when every sale goes through a registered marketplace facilitator like Amazon ([CDTFA Publication 109](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/formspubs/pub109/online-marketplaces-and-fulfillment-centers.htm)). To buy inventory without paying sales tax, give each supplier a resale certificate (form CDTFA-230) that explains why you hold no permit and lists the marketplace's permit number ([CDTFA Marketplace Facilitator guide](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/MPFAct.htm)). If a supplier insists on a permit number, registering is free at cdtfa.ca.gov, though CDTFA can ask for a security deposit.

**7. Check your city's business license rules.**
- Cost: varies by city.
- Why: many California cities want a license or tax certificate even for a home business. Claude can find your city's page.

**8. Keepa: start free, pay once you are calling brands.**
- Cost: free charts first. The paid plan is billed in euros; 2026 reviews list about €29 a month or €290 a year ([RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/keepa/pricing), checked 2026-11-12). Check keepa.com before you subscribe.
- Why: the whole method is reading Keepa's price, sales and seller charts. Week 1 needs only its free charts plus Amazon's free Revenue Calculator for fees. Other tools are optional.

**9. Connect Claude in Chrome.**
- Cost: $0.
- Why: so Claude can read Keepa charts and public Amazon product pages with you. It reads. You click anything inside Seller Central (see the safety lines in `06-ai-captains-for-amazon.md`).

**10. Insurance: know the two triggers.**
- Amazon requires commercial liability insurance of at least $1 million per occurrence within 30 days after your gross proceeds pass $10,000 in a month.
- Since 2026-11-02, products in Amazon's "enhanced safety" categories need that insurance **from the first sale**, whatever your sales. Amazon's examples include children's products, cosmetics, things people eat or swallow, and lithium battery products ([Novadata summary of Amazon's help page](https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-liability-insurance-enhanced-safety-categories-november-2026), 2026-11; check Amazon's own page in Seller Central, "Amazon commercial liability insurance requirements").
- For a beginner: stay out of those categories until you have a policy. Get quotes before you need one.

## Done when

Steps 1 to 9 are finished (the free Keepa is enough to start) and Seller Central shows your account as active. `/product-check` needs steps 8 and 9, so do those on day one; it does not need Seller Central yet.
